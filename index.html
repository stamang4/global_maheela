Who we are

People deserve pathways, not just programs.

Global Maheela exists to connect capability with opportunity, and opportunity with sustainable pathways for growth. We see people as creators, entrepreneurs, leaders and builders of their own futures.

Our model

Train. Create. Heal. Sell.

Four essential elements of sustainable empowerment.

01

Train

Practical knowledge and skills across digital literacy, finance, entrepreneurship, leadership and technology.

Capability → confidence → opportunity
02

Create

Support pathways toward products, services, micro-enterprises, businesses and community projects.

Skills → creation → economic activity
03

Heal

Wellbeing, reflection, community spaces, confidence-building and supportive networks.

Wellbeing → stability → growth
04

Sell

Connect appropriate livelihood initiatives and products with real customers and broader markets.

Creation → market → opportunity
